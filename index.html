Lead magnet · attempt-log QA · not counsel

A field checklist on the attempt notes, before anyone files.

SERVICE is an educational pack. You paste (or load) a process-server attempt log. The page maps common affidavit fields and marks what looks present or missing. It is paper QA on the notes. It is not a process-server, not a court filing, and it does not claim service was legally sufficient.

What it does

Attempt log in. Field gaps out. You still own the filing.

Features are checklist hygiene, not a sufficiency opinion. Benefits are fewer bounced affidavits from blank times and blank addresses if a human actually fills the gaps.

Features

  • Paste notes or load a synthetic three-attempt sample.
  • Eight affidavit-field rows: date/time, address tried, manner, recipient, diligence, papers, server ID, outcome.
  • GAP / PASS chips with educational field labels, not legal cites as holdings.
  • Watermarked pack preview. Book intro or email to talk through the pack. Magnet Stripe HOLD.
  • Browser-only. Nothing uploads. Restart returns the desk to empty.

Benefits (soft)

  • See missing clock times and blank attempts before counsel redlines the return.
  • Same job as a sticky checklist, on this log, not a forgotten spreadsheet tab.
  • Refuse on empty or nonsense so the demo does not invent a clean affidavit.
  • Standalone pack if you only want the paper QA; Pilot aisle if you want the suite.
